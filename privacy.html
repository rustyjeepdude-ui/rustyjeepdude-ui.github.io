SysAssist privacy policy

Updated 2 October 2026

Who it is for

SysAssist is a private assistant with one user, its owner, running on the owner's own computer and phone. It reads only the owner's own Google account, after the owner signs in.

What it reads (read-only)

  • YouTube (youtube.readonly): the owner's playlists, liked videos and video titles.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly), Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and Google Contacts (contacts.readonly): to answer the owner's own questions.

What it never does

  • It never changes, sends or deletes anything in the Google account.
  • It never sells, rents or shares the data, and shows no ads.
  • SysAssist does not use the data to train AI models.

Where the data goes

Data read from Google is kept on the owner's own computer and phone. To answer the owner's own questions, parts of it may be sent to the AI service the owner uses (the Google Gemini API). Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SysAssist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with Disconnect in SysAssist. Deleting SysAssist's files on the owner's computer removes the copies kept there.
