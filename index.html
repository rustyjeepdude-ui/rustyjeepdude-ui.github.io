SysAssist

SysAssist is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC and phone. It is not offered to the public and has a single user: its owner.

It signs in with Google only to read the owner's own Google data, read-only: YouTube playlists and liked videos (for its Entertainment tab), and Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts (for the assistant's answers).
